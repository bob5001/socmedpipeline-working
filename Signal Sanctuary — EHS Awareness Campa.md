# Signal Sanctuary — EHS Awareness Campaign

## Purpose
Create a top-of-funnel awareness campaign that helps people recognize subtle environmental patterns in their own experience — without diagnosis, fear, or claims.

This campaign is designed to:
- invite self-recognition,
- encourage curiosity,
- gently lead interested users toward the EHS awareness quiz,
- preserve Signal Sanctuary’s credibility-first tone.

Quotes and testimonials remain essential, but are positioned **after discovery**, not as the discovery mechanism itself.

---

## Core Principle
**Recognition before explanation.**

We do not tell people what they are experiencing.
We ask whether they have noticed patterns.

---

## Campaign Tone
- Calm
- Observational
- Non-alarmist
- Non-medical
- Credible
- Human

Avoid:
- diagnostic language
- fear framing
- energetic or spiritual metaphors
- claims of causality

---

## Content Pillars

### 1. Pattern Recognition
Broad, experiential prompts that invite reflection without labeling.

Examples:
- noticing changes across environments
- subtle physical sensations
- differences in sleep, clarity, or comfort

These posts are designed to be:
- highly shareable
- DM-worthy
- relatable across belief systems

---

### 2. Normalization
Reassures users that noticing patterns:
- doesn’t require certainty
- isn’t unusual
- doesn’t imply something is “wrong”

These posts build trust and reduce resistance.

---

### 3. Gentle Orientation (CTA-light)
Soft invitations to explore further:
- “There’s a short awareness quiz in our bio”
- “Learning starts with noticing”

No urgency.
No conversion pressure.

---

## Role of the Quiz
The quiz acts as a **bridge**, not a funnel:
- transforms curiosity into engagement
- frames awareness without diagnosis
- respects uncertainty

---

## Relationship to Testimonials
Testimonials:
- validate experience
- build credibility
- support consideration

Awareness content:
- earns attention
- creates entry
- widens reach

Both are required.
They serve different psychological moments.

---

## Success Signals
- saves
- shares
- DMs
- comments that begin with “this resonates” or “I’ve noticed this too”

Not:
- immediate sales
- debate
- persuasion

---

## Summary (Internal)
This campaign allows Signal Sanctuary to lead with curiosity and credibility, drawing people in through recognition rather than claims, and setting the stage for deeper engagement through testimonials and lived experience.
