Key Reported Symptoms of EHS
Neurological/Cognitive: Headaches, dizziness, fatigue, sleep disorders (insomnia), memory loss, and concentration difficulties.
Skin: Tingling, prickling, burning sensations, and rashes.
Physical/Musculoskeletal: Muscle aches and pain.
Cardiovascular/Other: Heart palpitations, nausea, and tinnitus (ringing in ears).
